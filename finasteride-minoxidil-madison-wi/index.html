    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Madison, WI

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Madison

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. With less DHT around, the follicles on the crown and temples stop shrinking as quickly, and many hold their size for years instead of fading out. Minoxidil adds something different. It keeps hairs in their growing phase longer, so they come in thicker.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. Early treatment keeps the most hair, since long-bald spots seldom respond. Some women past menopause use it off-label. Women who could become pregnant cannot.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Madison

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil, often 1.25 to 2.5 mg, is an off-label daily pill some men prefer to avoid the mess. One spray daily also works. The combination spray is applied to the scalp each day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. The early shed is common and usually temporary. Around month six, density starts to improve. Long-term trials showed most men held their hair for years. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Madison

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. Most of these fade after stopping, and mood changes need prompt reporting. Scalp dryness is the usual topical issue. Oral minoxidil can cause body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat. The spray may reduce systemic side effects.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. Expect questions about your medication list and family plans, because both can shape the choice. PSA results drop by about half. Pregnant partners should not touch broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Madison

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. When finasteride falls short, dutasteride is the usual next step, off-label for hair. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is topical-only. Minoxidil alone helps, but it does nothing about DHT.
